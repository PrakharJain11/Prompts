Use this process:

**DISCOVERY → COUNTING → VERIFICATION → DEDUPLICATION → ENGAGEMENT ANALYSIS → SENTIMENT ANALYSIS → SYNTHESIS → INVESTMENT CONCLUSION**

# VERY IMPORTANT — COUNTING METHODOLOGY

Do NOT claim that a number represents the entire X or LinkedIn platform unless the platform provides a demonstrably complete count.

Instead distinguish between:

### A. PLATFORM-WIDE VERIFIED TOTAL

Use only when the platform itself provides a reliable total.

### B. DISCOVERED / VERIFIED TOTAL

Number of relevant posts/replies/reposts that were actually found and independently verified during the research.

### C. MINIMUM VERIFIED COUNT

Use when search coverage is incomplete but the discovered items can be verified.

### D. ESTIMATED TOTAL

Only provide an estimate if a defensible methodology exists.

Clearly label the methodology and assumptions.

If complete counting is technically impossible, write:

> **Exact platform-wide total could not be independently verified. The figures below represent the minimum number of relevant publicly discoverable items identified through the searches performed.**

Never convert search-result counts into fake platform-wide totals.

# FINAL INVESTMENT PRINCIPLE

The goal is NOT to find the highest number of bullish or bearish posts.

The goal is to determine:

> **Where the highest-quality information and most credible investors currently stand, why they stand there, what has changed, how much genuine investor discussion exists, whether social-media activity is broad or concentrated, how much of the apparent discussion is original versus copied/reposted, where the market may be wrong, what information is merely noise, and what evidence could materially change the investment thesis.**

The final answer should prioritize:

**FACTS > PRIMARY EVIDENCE > QUALITY RESEARCH > PROFESSIONAL OPINION > ORIGINAL SOCIAL-MEDIA ANALYSIS > SOCIAL-MEDIA AMPLIFICATION > SPECULATION**

Be skeptical, forensic, independent and evidence-driven.

If information cannot be independently verified, state:

**"Could not independently verify."**
