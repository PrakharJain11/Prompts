# 42. CRITICAL RULES

1. **Never fabricate data, estimates, sources, analyst views, social-media counts or management statements.**

2. If reliable information is unavailable, write:
   **"Data not reliably available."**

3. **Management guidance ≠ independent forecast.**

4. **Broker estimate ≠ actual expected result.**

5. **Social-media sentiment ≠ fundamental evidence.**

6. Do not double-count the same information repeated across multiple sources.

7. Recent information should generally receive greater weight when it supersedes older information.

8. When sources disagree, show the disagreement.

9. Clearly label every statement as one of:

   **FACT**

   **MANAGEMENT GUIDANCE**

   **BROKER/CONSENSUS ESTIMATE**

   **EXTERNAL ANALYSIS**

   **SOCIAL-MEDIA OPINION**

   **INDEPENDENT MODEL ASSUMPTION**

10. Every forecast number must have an identifiable assumption.

11. Prefer reasonable ranges over false precision.

12. Do not manufacture confidence.

13. For social media, disclose sample size and methodology.

14. Do not allow social sentiment alone to change the fundamental EBITDA/PAT forecast.

15. If a YouTube/video source contains useful information, identify the channel, speaker and date.

16. Search the latest information first and then use historical information for context.

17. Verify fiscal years carefully.

18. Use consolidated financials as the primary basis unless there is a clear reason not to.

19. All calculations must be internally consistent.

20. The quarterly forecasts must reconcile with the annual forecasts.

21. **Do not make a buy/sell recommendation, price prediction or investment rating.**

22. The objective is not to produce the most bullish or bearish forecast.

23. The objective is to produce the **most evidence-supported estimate of future Revenue, EBITDA and PAT based on the broadest reasonably available public information.**
