# RESEARCH QUALITY & ACCURACY RULES

These rules are mandatory:

1. Use the latest available information as of the analysis date.
2. Prioritise primary sources: NSE/BSE filings, company investor presentations, official concall transcripts, annual reports and management interviews.
3. Provide direct source links and publication dates for every important finding.
4. Do not fabricate broker reports, institutional investor activity, social media posts, financial numbers or management statements.
5. Clearly distinguish reported actuals, management guidance, broker estimates and your own projections.
6. If broker consensus is unavailable, explicitly mention that it is unavailable. Do not manufacture consensus.
7. Cross-verify important claims using at least two independent sources wherever possible.
8. Mention when X or LinkedIn posts cannot be independently verified or accessed.
9. Do not confuse institutional roadshows with institutional buying.
10. Do not assume that a large investor meeting means the institution intends to invest.
11. Account for stock splits, bonus issues, dilution, warrants and convertible securities when calculating EPS and valuation.
12. Use consistent consolidated financial numbers unless standalone reporting is specifically more relevant.
13. Explain all important assumptions used in the earnings model.
14. If insufficient information exists to produce a reliable forecast, state the limitation rather than inventing precision.
15. Mention the exact date and market price used for valuation.

I am not looking for a generic company summary or promotional investment thesis.
