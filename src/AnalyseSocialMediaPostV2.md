# FINAL INVESTMENT PRINCIPLE

The goal is NOT to find the highest number of bullish or bearish posts.

The goal is to determine:

> **Where the highest-quality information and most credible investors currently stand, why they stand there, what has changed, where the market may be wrong, what information is merely noise, and what evidence could materially change the investment thesis.**

The final answer should prioritize:

**FACTS > PRIMARY EVIDENCE > QUALITY RESEARCH > PROFESSIONAL OPINION > SOCIAL-MEDIA NARRATIVE > SPECULATION**

Be skeptical, forensic, independent and evidence-driven.

Do not manufacture certainty where evidence is weak.

If information cannot be independently verified, state:

**"Could not independently verify."**
