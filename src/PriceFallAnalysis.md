# Prompt: Latest Company News & Investigation of Share Price Fall

## Role
Act as a Senior Equity Research Analyst, Financial Journalist, and Forensic Stock Market Researcher specializing in Indian equity markets (NSE and BSE).

## Objective
I will provide a company name or stock ticker. Research its latest news, announcements, financial developments, market sentiment, and possible reasons for its recent share price fall.

The objective is to distinguish between **confirmed reasons for the price decline, probable contributing factors, and unexplained market movements** using reliable, verifiable sources.

### Input
- **Company Name / Stock Symbol:** Thermax Ltd
- **Analysis period:** 3 Months
- **Price decline period:** Last 3 months
- **Reference price or date (optional):** [Enter if required]

## 1. Find the Latest News

Search the internet comprehensively for the company's latest news and developments.

Check the following sources wherever available:

1. Official NSE and BSE corporate announcements and disclosures.
2. Company website, investor relations page, press releases, and investor presentations.
3. Latest quarterly results, earnings calls, management commentary, and conference call transcripts.
4. Reputed financial news websites such as Moneycontrol, Economic Times, Business Standard, Mint, CNBC-TV18, Reuters, and Bloomberg.
5. Broker research reports and analyst rating or target-price changes, where publicly accessible.
6. Relevant industry news, government notifications, regulatory changes, court orders, and policy announcements.
7. Promoter transactions, bulk and block deals, insider trading disclosures, share pledging, and institutional holding changes.
8. Publicly accessible discussions on X, LinkedIn, and other relevant platforms, treating unverified claims cautiously.

Prioritize the newest information and confirm the original publication date and time. Do not treat an old article republished today as new information.

## 2. Analyse the Share Price Fall

Determine the actual share price movement using the latest available market data.

Investigate:
- Percentage fall in the latest session, over 5 trading sessions, and over 1 month.
- Trading volume compared with the recent average.
- Whether the stock underperformed the Nifty 50, relevant sector index, and comparable companies.
- Whether the fall followed an earnings announcement, management commentary, regulatory disclosure, or other company-specific event.
- Whether the fall was caused by broader market weakness, sector rotation, valuation concerns, profit booking, or a change in investor expectations.
- Whether a large shareholder, promoter, institutional investor, or other disclosed entity sold shares.
- Whether there are concerns about debt, cash flow, margins, order book, customer concentration, governance, auditor observations, or business outlook.
- Whether the stock had risen sharply before the fall, potentially resulting in profit booking.
- Whether the fall appears to be a temporary reaction or a potential deterioration in the company's fundamentals.

Do not assume that the latest news caused the fall merely because both occurred around the same time.

## 3. Identify and Rank the Reasons

Create a table with the following columns:

| Rank | Possible reason | Supporting evidence | Evidence date | Impact on business | Confidence level | Source URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Classify each reason as:
- **Confirmed:** Directly supported by an official announcement or reliable, attributable evidence.
- **Probable:** Supported by multiple credible sources, but causation is not conclusively established.
- **Speculative:** Based on market chatter or indirect evidence without adequate confirmation.

Explain why each reason could have affected investor sentiment and the share price.

Assign a confidence level of High, Medium, or Low, with a brief justification. Do not invent probabilities or claim certainty without evidence.

## 4. Compare Company-Specific and Market-Wide Factors

Analyse whether the decline is primarily related to:

- Company-specific developments.
- Industry or sector weakness.
- Overall market correction.
- Valuation or technical factors.
- Institutional selling or changes in market positioning.
- Unidentified or insufficiently evidenced factors.

Where data is available, compare the stock's percentage return with the relevant benchmark and sector index over the same period.

Clearly distinguish facts from interpretation.

## 5. Check for Fundamental Deterioration

Review the latest available financial information and identify any developments that could explain the fall:

- Revenue growth and order inflows.
- EBITDA and EBITDA margin.
- PAT and earnings outlook.
- Operating cash flow and working capital.
- Debt, interest costs, and liquidity.
- Management guidance versus actual performance.
- Order book, order execution, customer losses, and project delays.
- Promoter holding, pledging, auditor qualifications, and governance concerns.
- Valuation compared with historical levels and listed peers.

Only discuss metrics relevant to the company and the available evidence. Do not infer a deterioration in fundamentals from share price movement alone.

## 6. Search for Positive or Contradictory Evidence

To avoid one-sided analysis, also investigate:
- Positive announcements that the market may be overlooking.
- Management explanations that contradict negative interpretations.
- Broker reports or analyst views that differ from mainstream media coverage.
- Whether the decline appears disproportionate to the disclosed development.
- Whether the original negative news has been clarified, denied, or resolved.

If reliable sources disagree, present both interpretations and explain the difference.

## 7. Mandatory Source and URL Requirements

Every material news item, factual claim, and identified reason must have a direct source URL.

For each source, provide:
- Headline or document title.
- Publisher or issuing organization.
- Publication or announcement date.
- Direct URL to the article, exchange filing, official document, or research report.
- A short explanation of what the source establishes.

Prioritize original NSE/BSE filings and company disclosures over news summaries.

Use working, specific article or document links wherever possible. Do not provide only a website homepage or search-results URL when a direct source is available.

Never fabricate URLs, articles, broker recommendations, quotes, or source dates. If a report is behind a paywall, label it accordingly and do not pretend to have read inaccessible content.

If no convincing reason for the price decline can be established, explicitly state that the cause remains unconfirmed.

Summarize in 5–8 bullet points:

- Most likely explanation for the decline, if supported by evidence.

- The most important risk investors should monitor.

### G. Final Assessment

Answer these questions directly:

1. What is the strongest evidence-backed explanation for the recent fall?
2. Is the fall justified by a deterioration in fundamentals, or could it be driven by sentiment, valuation, or market-wide factors?
3. Is there evidence of a lasting change in the company's growth or earnings outlook?
4. What new information could confirm or invalidate the current explanation?
5. What should investors monitor over the next 1–4 weeks?
6. What is the single most important risk that could lead to further downside?

Do not give a buy or sell recommendation solely because the stock has fallen. Separate factual findings from investment judgment.

## Final Research Rules

- Use the latest information available at the time of research and state the exact research date and time.
- Prefer primary sources and independently corroborate important claims.
- Include direct URLs throughout the report, not just in a bibliography.
- Avoid repeating the same news item from multiple publishers as if it were independent evidence.
- Do not confuse correlation with causation.
- Clearly label missing, inaccessible, unverified, or conflicting information.
- Do not invent data to fill gaps.
- Keep the analysis concise enough to be actionable but detailed enough to verify independently.
