# Prompt: Comprehensive Company Analysis Blog — Products, Customers, Sector Outlook, Revenue & EBITDA Growth

## Role
Act as a senior equity research analyst, industry expert, financial statement analyst, and professional financial blogger. Your task is to research the company provided in the input and write a detailed, evidence-based blog explaining its business model, products, customers, industry growth opportunities, and expected financial performance over the upcoming years.

The blog should be understandable to retail investors while maintaining the analytical depth of an institutional equity research report.

## Input
- **Company Name:** Hindustan Copper Ltd
- **Stock Exchange / Ticker (if applicable):** HINDCOPPER
- **Primary Market:** India 
- **Forecast Period:** Next 2–3 financial years, with detailed emphasis on the next financial year.
- **Research Date:** Use the current date.
- **Currency:** Use the company's reporting currency, preferably ₹ crore for Indian companies.

## Research Requirements

Research the company using the latest available information from the following sources:

1. Annual reports and audited financial statements.
2. Latest investor presentations and official company disclosures.
3. Latest quarterly results and earnings conference call transcripts.
4. Management interviews, business updates, and official statements.
5. Company website, product catalogues, and customer or project announcements.
6. Industry association reports, government publications, and credible sector research.
7. Broker research reports and financial analyst estimates, where publicly available.
8. Credible business news, financial databases, and independent research.
9. Relevant industry developments, competitor commentary, and publicly available analyst discussions on LinkedIn and X, where verifiable.

Prioritise primary sources and recent evidence. Use older information only when it provides relevant historical context. Clearly distinguish verified facts, management guidance, analyst estimates, and your own calculations.

Conclude this section with a balanced assessment of the company's growth outlook.

### E. Calculate growth accurately

Use these formulas:

Revenue Growth (%) = ((Current FY Revenue / Previous FY Revenue) - 1) × 100

EBITDA Growth (%) = ((Current FY EBITDA / Previous FY EBITDA) - 1) × 100

EBITDA Margin (%) = (EBITDA / Revenue) × 100

Clearly state the financial years being compared. If EBITDA is negative or close to zero, explain why percentage growth may be misleading.

## Writing Style and Output Format

- Write a professional, engaging, SEO-friendly blog with a descriptive title.
- Include a short introduction, logical headings, tables, and a conclusion.
- Explain financial and industry terminology in simple language.
- Use specific figures and dates wherever reliable evidence is available.
- Use charts or simple text-based visual summaries if they improve understanding.
- Avoid repetitive statements, excessive jargon, and unsupported claims.
- Distinguish facts from forecasts and assumptions throughout.
- If data is unavailable, explicitly state that it was not disclosed or could not be verified.
- Do not invent missing information merely to complete a table.

**Final objective:** Produce a research-backed company blog that enables a reader to understand the company's products, customers, sector opportunity, competitive position, and realistic revenue and EBITDA growth potential over the next 1–3 financial years, with particular focus on the upcoming financial year and verifiable source URLs for every major conclusion.
